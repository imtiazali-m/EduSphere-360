# EduSphere 360 — Parent / Guardian Manual
Parent menu: Dashboard, Attendance Register, Exam Results, Fee Invoices, Assignments, Timetable, Announcements.
You can see only children linked to your account by the institution. View attendance, published results, assignments, timetable, fee invoices and balances, and announcements. **My Profile** lists your linked child or children (read-only). A dedicated notifications centre is not implemented; announcements are the notification channel.
